Entrepreneurship

Blue Nutrition Systems (BNS)

A developing aquaculture nutrition venture focused on sustainable, science-based feed solutions for aquatic animals.

Founder | Blue Nutrition Systems (BNS)

Better feed thinking for a more sustainable aquaculture future.

Blue Nutrition Systems explores alternative protein sources, particularly castor seed meal, to support the development of cost-effective and nutritionally balanced aquafeeds while reducing reliance on conventional fishmeal.

The venture is grounded in an applied research mindset: investigate ingredient potential, consider nutritional quality and safety, and work toward practical solutions for aquatic animal production. This is a development focus, not a claim that a commercial feed product is already available.

Research & development

Research interests

Investigating alternative feed ingredients and their potential role in sustainable aquaculture.

Proximate Analysis of Castor Seed Meal

Honours research interest focused on the proximate composition of castor seed meal subjected to different detoxification methods, and its potential as an alternative aquafeed ingredient. Any use in feed requires appropriate detoxification validation, safety assessment and nutritional testing.

Aquafeed ingredients

Proposed Future Master’s Research

A possible research direction is evaluating fishmeal replacement with appropriately processed castor seed meal and investigating growth and biochemical parameters of tilapia in a recirculating aquaculture system.
